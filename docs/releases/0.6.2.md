# Codex Pulse 0.6.2

- 修复 TPS 在响应缺少计时时反复消失的问题，保留上次有效读数。
- 长对话中途启动或恢复读取后，可从后续完整响应恢复采样，无需重新发送消息。
- 修复单次异常计时导致本轮后续响应持续无法测量的问题。
- 工具执行较久时，上次 TPS 不再因超过 60 秒而消失。历史读数继续使用灰色和 `~`，详情显示响应时间；轮次结束、断线或暂停后及时撤去。
- 多会话增减及许可等待变化时减少读数闪回；首次尚无读数时显示「等待新响应」。

## English

- Keep the last valid TPS reading when a response has no usable model timing.
- Resume measurement from subsequent complete responses when joining a long-running conversation, without sending another message.
- Recover after an individual response has invalid timing instead of losing measurement for the rest of the turn.
- Keep historical readings during long tool operations rather than discarding them after 60 seconds. Muted color, `~`, and response timestamps distinguish history; ended turns, disconnections, and pauses still clear readings.
- Reduce flicker when active conversations or permission waits change. Before the first reading, details show “Waiting for a new response”.
