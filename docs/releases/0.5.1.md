# Codex Pulse 0.5.1

## 简体中文

- 刷新时保留上一份已确认的数字、额度条和详情，避免同账户初始化通知反复把界面变成占位符；首次加载、确认换号或退出登录仍显示对应状态。
- 新数据返回后，百分比和充值余额使用约 360 ms 的原生数字过渡；减少动态效果时直接更新。
- 胶囊显示且连接正常时复用只读 App Server，减少重复启动及账户读取等待；隐藏后空闲 10 秒、失败、休眠或退出时释放连接。
- 保留账户隔离、断网 / 过期提示和现有刷新频率。复用连接期间会保留服务内存，实际网络请求仍有延迟。

## English

- Keep the last confirmed values, quota tracks and details visible during refreshes. Same-account initialization notifications no longer replace them with placeholders; initial loading and confirmed account changes retain their appropriate states.
- Animate confirmed percentage and credit updates with a short native numeric transition, or update directly when Reduce Motion is enabled.
- Reuse the read-only App Server while the capsule is visible and healthy. Release it after 10 idle seconds when hidden, after failures, and on sleep or exit.
- Preserve account isolation, offline and stale-data warnings, and the existing polling frequency. Connection reuse retains service memory; network requests still take time.
