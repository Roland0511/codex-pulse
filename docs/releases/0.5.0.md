# Codex Pulse 0.5.0

## 简体中文

- 任一订阅额度窗口耗尽、且同一额度桶仍有充值余额时，胶囊自动切换显示剩余点数；刷新确认订阅恢复或点数耗尽后切回百分比。
- 中英文统一使用 `60k`、`1.2M` 等紧凑数字，不追加单位。54 pt 小胶囊按实际字体宽度调整精度和字号，避免数字超框；悬停和详情保留完整余额。
- 点数模式保留工作特效和全部订阅窗口详情；细线表示有可用余额，不表示剩余百分比。
- 余额按额度桶隔离，缺失或非法字段保持未知；连接异常、过期和服务限制仍明确显示。只读官方本地额度接口，不执行充值、重置或账户修改。

## English

- Automatically show remaining credits when any subscription window is exhausted and the same quota bucket reports a positive credit balance. Return to percentages after a refresh confirms subscription recovery or depleted credits.
- Use compact values such as `60k` and `1.2M` in every language, without a trailing unit. Measure the native font to fit the 54 pt capsule; hover or expand for the full balance.
- Preserve work effects and all subscription-window details. The credit-mode line indicates available credits, not a remaining percentage.
- Keep balances isolated by quota bucket and missing or invalid fields unknown. Preserve connection, stale-data and service-limit warnings. Read the official local quota interface without purchasing credits, resetting limits or changing the account.
